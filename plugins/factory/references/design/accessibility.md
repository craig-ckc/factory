# Accessibility

- Prefer native semantics and controls. Verify accessible names, keyboard paths, visible focus, and sensible focus restoration.
- Check form labels/errors, dynamic state announcements, zoom/text resizing, target accessibility, and screen-reader information when relevant.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid essential meaning delivered only through animation.
- Treat established accessibility criteria as observable quality checks, not taste. Report what was measured or exercised and any unverified conditions.
